# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
This project presents a comprehensive HR Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI. The dashboard provides a 360° view of workforce health, focusing on attrition, employee satisfaction, performance, and compensation trends.

The goal of this project is to help HR stakeholders identify key drivers of attrition, evaluate employee experience, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

##  Objectives
- Monitor overall workforce metrics
- Analyze attrition patterns and root causes
- Evaluate employee satisfaction and performance
- Assess compensation fairness and career growth
- Provide actionable insights for HR strategy

---

## 🗂️ Dashboard Structure

### 🔵 Page 1: Executive Summary
Provides a high-level overview of workforce metrics.

**Key KPIs:**
- Total Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate (%)
- Average Age
- Average Monthly Income
- Average Experience

**Insights:**
- Attrition trends across departments
- Workforce distribution (Age, Job Level, Gender)
- Impact of overtime on attrition

---

### 🟣 Page 2: Attrition Root Cause Analysis
Deep dive into why employees are leaving.

**Key Visuals:**
- Top Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Business Travel
- Attrition by Salary Slab
- Attrition by Age Group & Marital Status
- Decomposition Tree (Root Cause Analysis)

**Insights:**
- High attrition observed in specific job roles
- Frequent travel and lower salary bands linked to higher attrition
- Early tenure employees more likely to leave

---

### 🟢 Page 3: Employee Satisfaction & Performance
Analyzes employee experience and performance alignment.

**Key Visuals:**
- Job Satisfaction by Department
- Environment Satisfaction by Job Role
- Work-Life Balance Distribution
- Relationship Satisfaction
- Performance vs Salary Hike (Combo Chart)

**Insights:**
- Satisfaction varies across departments
- Higher performance does not always correlate with lower attrition
- Training has moderate impact on performance

---

### 🟡 Page 4: Compensation & Growth Analysis
Focuses on employee compensation and career progression.

**Key KPIs:**
- Average Income
- Average Salary Hike %
- Promotion Rate %
- Average Years Since Promotion
- Pay vs Performance Indicator

**Key Visuals:**
- Salary Distribution by Job Role
- Waterfall (Salary Growth by Job Level)
- Small Multiples (Salary Growth across Departments)
- Salary Hike vs Performance
- Gauge (Salary Hike vs Target)

**Insights:**
- Compensation varies significantly across roles
- Promotion delays observed in certain departments
- Salary growth patterns differ by department

---

### 🟤 Drillthrough Page: Employee Detail View
Provides detailed employee-level analysis.

**Features:**
- Employee table with key attributes
- Dynamic KPIs based on selection
- Attrition and Satisfaction breakdown
- Interactive filtering via drillthrough

---

## Key Features

✅ Interactive slicer panel (toggle button)  
✅ Drillthrough functionality for detailed analysis  
✅ Tooltip pages for enhanced insights  
✅ Decomposition tree for root cause analysis  
✅ Small multiples for comparative analysis  
✅ Conditional formatting for KPI indicators  
✅ What-if scenario (salary impact on attrition)

## Business Insights

- Attrition is highest among early-tenure employees and lower salary groups
- Roles with frequent travel show higher attrition
- Compensation and promotion delays influence employee retention
- Satisfaction levels vary across departments and impact engagement
- Performance does not always guarantee higher rewards

## How to Use

1. Use the slicer panel (top-right button) to filter data
2. Hover over visuals to see detailed tooltips
3. Use drillthrough (right-click) to view detailed employee data
4. Navigate across pages to explore different HR dimensions
